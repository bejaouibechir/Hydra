# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive. Harassment, insults and personal attacks
are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through
[hydraetl.com](https://hydraetl.com). Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove content or block participants who do not follow this code.
