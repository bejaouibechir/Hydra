# Say hello to hdrctl

Check what you just installed:

`hdrctl --version`{{exec}}

See every command it has:

`hdrctl --help`{{exec}}

`--help` / `-h` work the same way on any subcommand:

`hdrctl init --help`{{exec}}

hdrctl also speaks Spanish, one call at a time — nothing to configure, no restart:

`hdrctl --lang es --help`{{exec}}

`--lang` only affects the call it's attached to; the next command is back to English.

Click **Check** to continue.
